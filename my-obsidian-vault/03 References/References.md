Per the PARA note-taking methodology, the references folder is for source material: articles, clippings, PDFs, attachments — things you refer to but don't act on. If a note has no next step and belongs to no project, it probably lives here. Link references into projects and areas with `related-to` so they surface where you need them.

Check out the Obsidian Web Clipper add-on for browsers like Firefox/Chrome for quickly capturing things to your vault like articles or recipes.
